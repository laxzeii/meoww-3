1 · BODY

Three Main Parts

A beetle's body has three main regions:

  • Head: has the eyes, antennae, and mouth.
  • Thorax: supports the six legs and wings.
  • Abdomen: contains many internal organs.
  • Exoskeleton: the hard outer covering that protects the body.
2 · HEAD

Head Parts

The head helps the beetle sense its surroundings and eat.

  • Antennae: used for smell, touch, and sensing.
  • Compound eyes: help detect light and movement.
  • Mandibles: jaws used for biting and chewing.
  • Palps: help handle and sense food.
3 · THORAX & LEGS

Legs and Wings

The thorax carries the beetle's legs and wings.

  • Legs: each beetle has six legs for walking and climbing.
  • Femur: the strong middle part of the leg.
  • Tibia: the lower leg section.
  • Tarsus: the foot section with small claws.
  • Elytra: hard front wings that protect the delicate hind wings.
